## Exam Overview (UPSC CSE 2027)

- **Notification**: Expected 13 January 2027
- **Application**: ~13 Jan to 2 Feb 2027
- **Prelims**: 23 May 2027
- **Mains**: From 20 August 2027 (5 days)
- **Interview**: Early 2028
- **Final Merit**: Mains (1750) + Interview (275) = 2025 marks

### Stages
1. **Prelims** (Screening)
   - GS Paper I: 100 Q, 200 marks, 2 hrs (counts for cutoff)
   - CSAT Paper II: 80 Q, 200 marks, 2 hrs (qualifying 33%)
   - Negative marking: 1/3rd
2. **Mains** (Merit)
   - Paper A: Indian Language (qualifying 300)
   - Paper B: English (qualifying 300)
   - Essay: 250
   - GS I to IV: 250 each
   - Optional Paper I & II: 250 each
3. **Personality Test**: 275 marks

## Syllabus Snapshot (Core Areas for Daily Content)

**Prelims GS-I**: Current events, History of India & National Movement, Indian & World Geography, Indian Polity & Governance, Economic & Social Development, Environment/Ecology/Biodiversity/Climate Change, General Science.

**Mains GS-I**: Indian Heritage & Culture, Modern Indian History, Freedom Struggle, World History, Indian Society, Geography of India & World.

**GS-II**: Constitution, Polity, Governance, Social Justice, International Relations.

**GS-III**: Indian Economy, Agriculture, S&T, Environment, Disaster Management, Internal Security.

**GS-IV**: Ethics, Integrity, Aptitude, Case Studies.

**Essay**: Multiple topics – society, governance, economy, philosophy, environment etc.

Content builds these areas progressively with explanations, key facts for memorisation, interlinkages, and practice-oriented notes.
